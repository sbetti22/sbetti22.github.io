I am an astronomical data scientist at Space Telescope Science Institute and observational astronomer. I am the product owner and developer for Python-based open source visualization software supporting JWST and Roman. My astronomical research focuses on accretion mechanism from circumstellar disks onto young brown dwarfs & planetary mass companions, and icy dust grains in debris disks around young stars. I got my Ph.D. in Astronomy at UMass Amherst, and my B.S. in Astrophysics at Haverford College in Pennsylvania.

The Comprehensive Archive of Substellar and Planetary Accretion Rates (CASPAR) is the largest database of accretion rates to-date. These have been gathered from literature and self-consistently rederived producing a uniform catalog of brown dwarf, star, and planet physical and accretion properties.

database publication python analysis tools

Using JWST/NIRSpec, we have detected ices in debris disks for the first time! Using advanced high contrast imaging techniques, we are able to image the disks of young planet forming systems, and with the IFU capabilities of NIRSpec, extract a spectra of the reflected ice at every pixel in the image.
